Naija Life Privacy

The account service stores your email address, username, display name, password hash, account timestamps, and the character appearance you choose. Passwords are stored as salted hashes. Authentication uses a secure, HTTP-only session cookie.

The game currently saves detailed play progress in this browser on this device, scoped to the signed-in account. The server stores wallet, verified payment, and account profile records. Password reset emails are sent only when an email provider is configured.

Do not enter sensitive personal information in your username or game messages. This starter privacy notice must be reviewed and updated with the operator's contact details, retention rules, and applicable legal requirements before a public commercial launch.
