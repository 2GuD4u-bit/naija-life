Naija Life Terms

Naija Life is a fictional city life game. You must be at least 18 to create an account. Keep your password private and use an account name that does not impersonate another person or expose someone else's private information.

Game progress and virtual currency are for use in the game. They have no cash value and cannot be withdrawn. Do not use the game to harass, threaten, or share private information about real people.

Accounts may be suspended when needed to protect players or the service. These terms are a starter policy for this project and should be reviewed before a public commercial launch.
